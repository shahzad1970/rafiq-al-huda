Rafiq Al-Huda Support

Rafiq Al-Huda is a free Islamic learning companion for Qur'an study, prayer learning, du'a, hadith, prayer times and Qibla direction.

Contact

Email shahzad@cosmicholding.com for help, feedback or a privacy request.

Common help

  • Microphone access is required only for recitation listening and pronunciation practice.
  • Location access helps calculate prayer times and Qibla direction. Prayer location can also be set manually.
  • The optional on-device question-answering model requires a one-time download.
  • Practice history and other local app data can be removed from Settings.

Important notice

Automatic tajwid and pronunciation feedback can be wrong and does not replace a qualified Qur'an teacher.

Please include your iPhone model, iOS version and a short description of the issue when requesting technical help. Do not send private recordings unless specifically requested.
