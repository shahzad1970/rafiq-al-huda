Rafiq Al-Huda Privacy Policy

Effective September 23, 2026

Rafiq Al-Huda is designed to keep personal information and recitation activity on your device. The app does not require an account and does not include advertising or third-party analytics.

Microphone

Microphone access is used for on-device Qur'an recitation recognition and pronunciation practice. Raw microphone audio is processed ephemerally, is not uploaded, and is not permanently stored by default. A recording is saved only when you explicitly choose to save it, and saved recordings remain on your device.

Location and compass

Location and motion sensors are used on the device to calculate prayer times and Qibla direction. Location information is not sent to the developer. You may deny location access and set a prayer location manually.

Local app data

Bookmarks, settings, practice history and progress are stored locally on your device. The app provides controls to delete recordings, practice history and all local data.

Downloads and external connections

The app may connect to the internet when you choose to download approved models or reference resources. These requests are used to deliver the selected file and are not used by the developer to build a profile about you. Core learning and recognition features are designed to work offline after required resources are installed.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children or adults.

Changes

If the app's data practices change, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosure will be updated before the changed version is released.
