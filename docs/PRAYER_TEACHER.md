# Prayer Teacher — first lesson

Scope: a complete two-rakah voluntary-prayer learning sequence for someone praying
alone. Not a school-specific validity checklist or congregational-prayer guide.
No camera, microphone grading, automatic religious rulings or fabricated audio.
Do not label this first lesson a full four-school course.

The user requested school differences, rather than a single-school presentation.
The lesson shows selected differences in expandable notes. The four-school
finger summary is paraphrased from Shaykh Farid Dingle's comparison:
https://seekersguidance.org/answers/shafii-fiqh/how-to-understand-the-concept-of-raising-the-finger-during-tashahhud/

Other checked references:
- Order and calmness: https://sunnah.com/bukhari:757
- Opening wording: https://sunnah.com/abudawud:775
- Bow/prostration praise and rising: https://sunnah.com/muslim:772
- Sitting testimony: https://sunnah.com/muslim:402a
- Blessings: https://sunnah.com/bukhari:3370
- Dua before salam: https://sunnah.com/bukhari:834
- Two salams: https://sunnah.com/abudawud:996
- Tashahhud variants: https://seekersguidance.org/answers/shafii-fiqh/can-a-shafii-recite-the-hanafi-tashahhud/
- Raising hands: https://islamqa.org/shafii/seekersguidance-shafii/168659/should-i-avoid-raising-my-hands-when-praying-with-hanafis-shafii/
- Maliki salam: https://data.nur.nu/Kutub/English/Qayrawani_Risala-Maliki-Fiqh-alt-eng.pdf (chapter 10)

Quran verses are loaded unchanged from QuranRepository; English is the bundled
Rowwad/QuranEnc published translation. Other meanings are editorial paraphrases.
Posture diagrams are original schematic code drawings, not detailed instructions
for foot, finger or hand placement. They need teacher review before a polished
anatomical illustration set is commissioned.

Offline text and diagrams; bundled Fatiha audio. Other Quran audio uses the
existing AbdulBaset Mujawwad downloader/cache. Non-Quran phrases now have bundled
human recordings; provenance and remaining listening QA are in PRAYER_AUDIO.md.
