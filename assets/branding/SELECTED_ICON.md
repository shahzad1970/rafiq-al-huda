# Selected app icon — Emerald Pages (D)

The active Rafiq Al-Huda icon retains the ivory open book and dark emerald
background selected by the user. A warm-gold path rises from the book's centre
fold toward an eight-point guiding star, expressing “Companion of Guidance.”
The active Apple AppIcon catalog is generated from
`app_icon_rafiq_al_huda_master.png`, a 1024×1024 opaque production master.
iOS applies the rounded-corner mask itself.

The source was generated with the built-in image-generation tool. It contains
no text, border, transparency, or baked rounded corners.

The attached design was used directly, without regeneration or design changes.

The previous master and alternative previews remain available for reference.
